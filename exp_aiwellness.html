Lead Full-Stack Engineer — Tryme (Startup)

Feb 2026

Role & Responsibilities

As the lead full-stack engineer for Tryme, I developed a fitness + wellness AI where people with mental illness or depression can talk openly and get empathetic solutions, tips, and conversation. The AI responds with empathy, practical solutions, and helpful conversation guidance.

I built the entire application end-to-end – from the conversational AI experience to the full frontend, backend, and cloud infrastructure – making mental health support accessible, private, and always available.

Key Features & Technologies

  • Open-Minded AI Conversations: A safe, judgment-free space where users can talk openly about mental illness or depression.
  • Practical Solutions & Tips: The AI provides actionable solutions and guided conversation tips tailored to the user's state of mind.
  • Fitness & Wellness Focus: Combines mental wellness support with fitness guidance for a holistic health experience.
  • Empathetic AI Design: Conversation flows engineered to be supportive, calming, and clinically mindful.
  • Full-Stack & Cloud: Complete end-to-end development as the sole developer, covering frontend, backend, AI integration, and cloud deployment.
