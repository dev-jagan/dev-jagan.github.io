Lead Full-Stack Engineer — Maid Apps (Startup)

Jan 2026

Role & Responsibilities

As the lead full-stack engineer for Maid Apps, I developed two interconnected apps – one for maids and one for users – sharing a single DB with simultaneous real-time sync, so data stays perfectly in sync on each side at all times.

This architecture ensures that when a maid completes onboarding, their profile is instantly and correctly available on the users' app, enabling a smooth, automated matchmaking flow from day one.

Key Features & Technologies

  • Single Shared Database: One database connecting both the maid app and the users app for consistent data across the platform.
  • Real-Time Sync: Simultaneous live data synchronization between both apps, so updates on one side reflect instantly on the other.
  • Smooth Maid Onboarding: Streamlined onboarding flow where new maid profiles are automatically fixed and published to the users' app.
  • User Matching: Users can easily browse and connect with verified maid profiles that appear in real time.
  • Full-Stack & Cloud: Complete end-to-end development as the sole developer, covering frontend, backend, and cloud infrastructure.
